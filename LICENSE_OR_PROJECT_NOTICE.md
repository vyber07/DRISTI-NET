# Project notice

DRISHTI-NET prototype — hackathon / research prototype. All bundled data is synthetic and fictional.

Prohibited use: this software must not be connected to real personal data, live police, telecom, banking or surveillance
systems, or used to make or support determinations about real people, without a lawful basis, an approved purpose, and
independent review. It produces analytical candidates for authorized human review; it does not determine guilt.
