## 1. Statutory Alignment: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) 2023 s.63

In Indian criminal jurisprudence, digital records must satisfy strict technical admissibility criteria under Section 63(4)(c) of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) 2023 (formerly Indian Evidence Act Section 65B):

1. **Identifying Electronic Record:** The system outputs an unambiguous identifier, MIME type, file size, and SHA-256 hash.
2. **Device Description & Custody:** Reports cite the server operating environment, storage bucket location, upload source IP, and ingestion timestamp.
3. **Lawful Custody & Ordinary Operation:** Documents that the electronic record was produced by the computer system during the period over which the computer was used regularly to store or process information.
4. **Operating Condition & Integrity:** Demonstrates through ClamAV logs, checksum manifests, and HMAC audit chains that the storage and processing systems operated properly without unrecorded alterations.
5. **Technical Neutrality Disclaimer:** Dossier PDFs explicitly state that they are automated technical reference certificates of system integrity, not legal determinations of substantive guilt or court judgments.

## 2. Data Protection & Privacy Principles (DPDP Act 2023 Alignment)

- **Purpose Limitation:** Evidence files and derived claims are scoped strictly to the authorized investigation case and authorized jurisdiction.
- **Data Minimization:** Default masking of Aadhaar, PAN, phone numbers, and financial accounts prevents unnecessary incidental exposure to unauthorized users.
- **Accountability & Auditing:** Every inspection of sensitive identifiers is tracked in the immutable audit trail.
- **De-Identification & Synthetic Data Mandate:** Demonstrations, evaluations, and testing use synthetic data generated by `data/synthetic/generate.py`. Live personal data is strictly prohibited in test environments.
